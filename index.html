ABOUT SITA

A Community Built on Trust & Business

SITA brings together Sindhi businessmen, traders, shopkeepers, industrialists, manufacturers, professionals, seniors, retirees and singles — men and women aged 21 to 80 — from India and Sindhi families settled abroad.

🤝

Our Purpose

To nurture business relationships and mutual cooperation among Sindhi friends and families, so that trade, trust and opportunity flow freely within our own community.

🌍

A Global Sindhi Family

Whether you're in Pune, Pimpri or anywhere else in India — or part of the Sindhi diaspora abroad — SITA is your bridge to a wider circle of trusted business relationships.

💯

100% Business, Zero Timepass

SITA is a focused professional network — not a casual chat group. Every meeting, every connection is designed to create real business and personal value.

COMMUNITY GUIDELINES

A Serious Network, Built on Respect

SITA is a 100% professional business network — kept clean, focused and purposeful for every member.

✅ We Encourage

  • Genuine business, trade & personal networking among members
  • Building trust-based relationships within the community
  • Active participation in monthly chapter meetings
  • Bringing guests & visitors to grow the network

❌ Not Allowed

  • Political or religious posts, photos of deities
  • Stickers, forwards & timepass messages
  • Good Morning / Good Night broadcasts
  • Personal or family posts unrelated to business

⚠️ Important Disclaimer

Members may engage in financial, business, family or personal dealings with one another entirely at their own discretion and risk. Every member is fully responsible and accountable for their own decisions. SITA, its group, admins and organizers hold no responsibility or liability whatsoever for any transaction, dispute or dealing between members. Please exercise due diligence before entering into any arrangement.
